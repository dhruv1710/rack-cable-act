Datacentres Manipulation benchmark_

11 data-centre manipulation tasks in MuJoCo, with one robot model, one world and one Gymnasium interface

  • 9 single-arm, 1 two-arm, 1 mobile base tasks
  • Franka Panda arm. Control at 50 Hz. Cameras 192x256 RGB, thermal 120x160.
  • Runs in simulation (MuJoCo) to be expanded into IsaacSIM

Scripted policies

Reference solutions for 8 of the 11 tasks. They use inverse kinematics and privileged simulator state, except the patrol, which works from the thermal image only. They show a task is solvable and are the source for demonstration data. Videos are at 2x speed.

Limits

  • Simulation only.
  • Rendering is not photoreal. Cables and beads are rigid.
  • Scripted policies cover 8 of 11 tasks, 7 of them on at least two thirds of seeds (table above). They are not datasets yet.
  • Baseline has 3 episodes per task.
