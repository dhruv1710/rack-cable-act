Datacentres Manipulation benchmark_

11 data-centre manipulation tasks in MuJoCo, with one robot model, one world and one Gymnasium interface

  • 9 single-arm, 1 two-arm, 1 mobile base tasks
  • Franka Panda arm. Control at 50 Hz. Cameras 192x256 RGB, thermal 120x160.
  • Runs in simulation (MuJoCo) to be expanded into IsaacSIM

Scripted policies

Inverse kinematics on privileged simulator state, except the patrol, which uses the thermal image only. They show each task is solvable and are the source for demonstration data. Videos at 2x.

Where the scripts fail.

  • Drive replacement (3 / 9). The arm must hold the drive level all the way to the bay. The configuration it picks the drive up in cannot reach most bays, and it cannot change configuration while holding the drive.
  • Parts sorting (16 / 24). One or two bolts land short of or beside their compartment.
  • Relative placement (16 / 24). Mostly the mug and cereal box. The open gripper (8 cm) barely clears the 7.4 cm mug, and a fingertip on the rim knocks it over.
  • Bottle binning (10 / 12). One bottle is knocked off the belt onto the floor.
  • Pouring beads (21 / 24). Beads always reach the bowl. The cup tips over on the way back down.
  • Cable routing works only on a simplified layout: a straight 0.40 m cable with the clips on one line.

Limits

  • Simulation only. Rendering is not photoreal; cables and beads are rigid.
  • Scripted policies cover all 11 tasks: 6 succeed on every tested seed, the rest on 33% to 88%. They are not datasets yet.
  • The baseline has 3 episodes per task.
